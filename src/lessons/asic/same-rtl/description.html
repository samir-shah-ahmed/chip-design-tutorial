The module in the editor can go through either flow. The RTL does not say "FPGA" or "ASIC". The flows diverge after the simulator is happy.

QuestionFPGAASIC
What implements a +?LUTs and the carry chain, or a DSPStandard cells from the PDK
What is the clock?A global buffer fed by a pin or a PLLA clock tree built during CTS
How do you know the clock is fast enough?nextpnr or Gowin timing at the speed grade on the partSTA at several PVT corners on extracted wires
What leaves the tool?A bitstream (.fs on Gowin)GDSII plus the signoff reports
What does a mistake cost?Reprogram the Tang NanoA respin, unless you caught it before tapeout

Write the clock enable in the RTL either way. On the FPGA it is the alternative to gating the clock. On the ASIC it is what the clock-gate cell will be inferred from, if you ask synthesis to do that and the library has the cell. The simulator checks the enable. It does not check which flow you intended.

Exercise: Increment only when ce is high. Three enabled clocks should reach 3, and further clocks with ce low should hold 3.
