A digital circuit is a network of Boolean functions. The functions you will write in Verilog are the same ones a lookup table or a standard cell will later implement.

The operators that become gates

  • & AND, | OR, ^ XOR, ~ NOT
  • assign is continuous: the left-hand side tracks the expression, like a wire driven by a gate.

A 1-bit full adder adds a, b, and a carry-in. The sum bit is the parity of the three inputs. The carry-out is set when at least two inputs are 1. Those two equations are the entire circuit.
