Boolean algebra is the mathematics behind every logic gate, every processor, and every digital circuit ever made.

Fundamental Laws

Sum of Products (SOP)

Any Boolean function can be expressed as an OR of AND terms (minterms):

F = A'B'C + A'BC + ABC'
// In Verilog: assign f = (~a & ~b & c) | (~a & b & c) | (a & b & ~c);

Karnaugh Maps (K-Maps)

A visual tool for simplifying Boolean expressions. Group adjacent 1s in powers of 2 to find minimal SOP form:
