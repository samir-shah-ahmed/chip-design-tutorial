Power consumption determines how hot a chip gets, how long a battery lasts, and whether a design is even feasible. Modern chips are as much about managing power as they are about performance.

Types of Power

Power Reduction Techniques

  • Clock Gating — Disable clock to idle modules. Most effective technique (saves both power and area for clock distribution).
  • Voltage Scaling — P ∝ V². Reducing voltage by 20% saves ~36% dynamic power.
  • Power Gating — Completely shut off power to unused blocks (eliminates leakage).
  • Multi-Vt Libraries — Use high-threshold cells on non-critical paths (less leakage) and low-threshold on critical paths (faster).
  • DVFS — Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling. Adapts voltage and clock speed based on workload.

Clock Gating in Verilog

// Without clock gating (wastes power):
always @(posedge clk)
  if (enable) reg_out <= data_in;

// With clock gating (saves power):
wire gated_clk = clk & enable;  // simplified; real designs use ICG cells
always @(posedge gated_clk)
  reg_out <= data_in;
