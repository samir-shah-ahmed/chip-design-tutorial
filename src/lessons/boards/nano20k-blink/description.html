Same counter as the 9K blinky, on the 20K's pins. Sipeed's blink article puts the 27 MHz oscillator on pin 4 and LED0 on pin 15. LiteX names six active-low LEDs on pins 15 through 20. The module is six bits wide so it matches that bus; if you have only confirmed LED0, constrain bit 0 and leave the other bits off the CST until you have checked the schematic (nextpnr errors if a top-level port has no IO_LOC).

IO_LOC "clk" 4;
IO_PORT "clk" IO_TYPE=LVCMOS33;
IO_LOC "led[0]" 15;
IO_PORT "led[0]" IO_TYPE=LVCMOS33 PULL_MODE=UP;

Half a second at 27 MHz is the 13 499 999 count from that Sipeed article. Keep LIMIT at 4 while you simulate.
