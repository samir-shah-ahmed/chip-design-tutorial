The Tang Primer 20K is the core-board form of the GW2A-18, not the Tang Nano 20K stick. Sipeed's FAQ lists it as GW2A-18C, PBGA256, C8/I7. Apicula lists GW2A-LV18PG256C8/I7 with family GW2A-18, and separately warns that C-variant silicon wants family=GW2A-18C. Read the date code and Apicula's family note before you pick the flag. Getting this wrong produces a bitstream the chip rejects.

The Primer docks (and the Tang Mega boards) are larger than a Nano: more I/O, often DRAM on the dock, and a different connector. This pass does not include a dock pin table. A Mega 138K or 60K lesson is not here either. Use the dock schematic that shipped with the carrier, and the same Yosys, nextpnr-himbaechel, gowin_pack, and openFPGALoader sequence, with the device string for that part.

What does carry over from the Nano tracks is the RTL: blink, debounce, UART, PWM, and the line counter. Only the CST and the device string change. The exercise below is the simulator check, not a flashing procedure.
