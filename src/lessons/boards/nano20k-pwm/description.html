PWM turns a duty cycle into brightness on an LED, or into a crude analog level after a filter. A free-running counter compares against duty. Because the Tang Nano 9K LEDs are common-anode to 1.8 V (Sipeed/Lushay getting-started article), the pin is driven low to light the LED. The compare is inverted relative to a common-cathode LED.

Map led with the blinky CST for the board you are on. The 9K LED pins are 10, 11, 13, 14, 15, 16. The 20K blink article documents LED0 on pin 15; LiteX lists LED0 through LED5 on pins 15 through 20 and names them active-low.

Exercise: Drive led low when count < duty. With duty equal to 4, exactly 4 of 16 samples should be low.
