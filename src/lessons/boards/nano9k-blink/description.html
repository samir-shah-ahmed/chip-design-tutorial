This is the first design to put on a Tang Nano 9K: a counter that toggles the LED bus. The in-browser testbench uses LIMIT = 4 so you can see the toggle. At 27 MHz, a half-period of 0.5 s is 13 500 000 cycles, which is the count the Sipeed blink tutorials use (they count to 13 499 999).

LEDs are active-low. Reset drives them high (off). A toggle drives them low (on).

IO_LOC "clk" 52;
IO_PORT "clk" PULL_MODE=UP;
IO_LOC "led[0]" 10;
IO_LOC "led[1]" 11;
IO_LOC "led[2]" 13;
IO_LOC "led[3]" 14;
IO_LOC "led[4]" 15;
IO_LOC "led[5]" 16;

Flash with the commands in the Tang Nano 9K overview. The top module you synthesize is blink, and the testbench stays on the simulator.

Exercise: Toggle led when count hits LIMIT. The log should say PASS led toggled.
