The Tang Nano 20K has an HDMI connector. This lesson does not copy a TMDS pin table. The Sipeed 20K blink article, which was checked for this track, documents the 27 MHz clock on pin 4 and LED0 on pin 15, and does not list HDMI pairs. Take the pairs from the Tang Nano 20K schematic or from Sipeed's 20K example repository before you write a CST. Inventing them would program the wrong pins.

What does transfer from the 9K display lesson is the structure: a line counter, a pixel clock from a PLL or from the MS5351 (Sipeed's unbox notes put MS5351 CLK0/CLK1/CLK2 on FPGA pins 10, 11, and 13), and a TMDS serializer taken from a Sipeed example rather than written from memory. The exercise is the same line counter, so you can simulate the timing generator while the pin table is still on your desk.

Exercise: Assert hsync while x < H_SYNC.
